- **Reference Model Sheet**: `textures/characters/tiffa-model-sheet.png`

- **Jewelry**: Delicate silver/black cord necklace with a single round white circular pendant (`#FFFFFF`) sitting in the center of her collarbone.
- **Bottoms**: High-waisted black pleated tennis/A-line skirt (`#1E1E22`).
- **Legwear & Footwear**: White ruffled ankle socks (`#F6F5F2`) and glossy black Mary Jane shoes (`#191819`) with silver buckles and rounded toes.
- **Accessories**: Crossbody black shoulder bag with silver hardware and a distinctive white 4-leaf clover charm (`#FFFFFF`) dangling from the strap ring.
